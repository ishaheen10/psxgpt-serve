# Research from source context

A small outline identifies up to three independent questions. Questions that filter companies first use a bounded index of reported aggregates or raw PSX market capitalization. Every section shares that selection and the original question.

Each section gathers relevant filings, announcements, macro documents or public web evidence, then streams its own cited answer. Tagged-page discovery supplements full-text search. Market data retains its provider date and values. Web pages are read after discovery and their digests are labelled separately from original filings.

The active code is `apps/analysis/src/research/`. It bypasses full normalized statements, precomputed ratios, synthetic periods and financial-model builders. The initial aggregate index covers a partial March 2026 bank slice; missing coverage remains visible.

Progress shows the outline, actual work and next steps. Follow-ups can reuse saved sources. Transcription defects and missing reports must be disclosed; a citation alone does not establish factual correctness.

See `apps/analysis/spec.md` for the product contract and `apps/analysis/runtime.md` for execution limits and publication utilities.
